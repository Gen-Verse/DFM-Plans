# DFM Open Research Problems

Discovery Foundation Models remain largely an open research agenda. This page tracks problems that we believe are important, undersolved, and open for collaboration.

Projects may be **academic, open-source, domain-specific, or real-world applied**. Selected problems may receive research funding, compute, engineering, data, simulation, or experimental support.

## Capability Map

1. Problem Discovery
2. Problem Formulation
3. Representation Construction
4. Hypothesis Formation
5. Intervention & Experimentation
6. Evidence-Grounded Revision
7. Continual Discovery Improvement

Cross-cutting tracks: **DFM Infrastructure · Evaluation & Environments · Domain Discovery**

---

## Initial Open Problems

### OP-01 — Discovering Mis-Specified Problems
**Area:** Problem Discovery / Formulation  
**Horizons:** Digital, Simulation  
**Status:** Open · Seeking collaborators

Can a model recognize that the supplied problem itself is wrong, incomplete, proxy-driven, or non-identifiable rather than merely failing to solve it?

---

### OP-02 — Learning Representation Revision
**Area:** Representation Construction  
**Horizons:** Digital, Simulation, Physical  
**Status:** Open

How can a model learn when to add a latent variable, remove a proxy, change scale, separate regimes, or replace the current representation?

---

### OP-03 — Mechanistically Distinct Hypothesis Formation
**Area:** Hypothesis Formation  
**Horizons:** All  
**Status:** Open

How can systems generate hypotheses that differ in mechanism, assumptions, validity ranges, and intervention response rather than surface wording?

---

### OP-04 — Discriminating Intervention Design
**Area:** Intervention & Experimentation  
**Horizons:** Simulation, Physical  
**Status:** Open · Funding may be available

How should a DFM choose experiments for expected research-state change under feasibility, cost, uncertainty, and risk constraints?

---

### OP-05 — Evidence-Grounded Failure Attribution
**Area:** Revision  
**Horizons:** All  
**Status:** Open

How can a system distinguish theory failure from measurement error, implementation error, hidden confounding, protocol deviation, random noise, or environment shift?

---

### OP-06 — Scientific RL for Discovery Trajectories
**Area:** Capability Formation  
**Horizons:** Digital, Simulation  
**Status:** Open · Seeking collaborators

How should reinforcement learning assign long-horizon credit to problem reformulation, representation changes, experiments, negative results, and revisions?

---

### OP-07 — Process-Centered DFM Evaluation
**Area:** Evaluation  
**Horizons:** All  
**Status:** Open

Can we evaluate knowledge progress and discovery-process quality while controlling for memorization, retrieval, prompt reuse, evaluator exploitation, and additional compute?

---

### OP-08 — Explicit Research State Infrastructure
**Area:** Infrastructure  
**Horizons:** All  
**Status:** Open

Build inspectable research-state representations supporting branches, rollback, provenance, evidence links, uncertainty, budgets, and human interventions.

---

### OP-09 — Continual Discovery Skill Evolution
**Area:** Continual Discovery Improvement  
**Horizons:** Digital, Simulation, Recursive  
**Status:** Open · Funding may be available

How can systems extract reusable discovery operations from experience, validate their triggers and effects, avoid negative transfer, and safely revise or remove them?

---

### OP-10 — Interactive Discovery Environments
**Area:** Environments  
**Horizons:** Digital, Simulation  
**Status:** Open

Design environments that hide scientifically meaningful structure while exposing external consequences, counterfactual interventions, and process-level evaluation signals.

---

### OP-11 — Bring a Scientific Domain to DFM
**Area:** Domain Discovery  
**Horizons:** Any  
**Status:** Open · Partnerships welcome

We welcome domain experts who can contribute real problems, data, simulators, protocols, validators, experimental infrastructure, or wet-lab access.

---

### OP-12 — Challenge the DFM Framework
**Area:** Foundations / Reproduction  
**Horizons:** Any  
**Status:** Open

Find counterexamples, missing capabilities, conflicting assumptions, non-reproducible claims, or settings where the current DFM formulation breaks.

---

## Propose a New Problem

Open an issue using the **Research Proposal** or **Domain Proposal** template. Strong proposals should specify:

- the research bottleneck,
- why existing task-solving or search formulations are insufficient,
- what evidence would constitute progress,
- what environment or data is required,
- and what would falsify the proposed direction.

For funded problems, domain collaborations, industry challenges, or non-public research opportunities, contact **[yang@phai-labs.com](mailto:yang@phai-labs.com)**.

> **Pick a problem. Build a missing piece. Bring a new domain.**
