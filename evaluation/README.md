# Evaluation

**Status: Planned**

This direction studies how to evaluate discovery capabilities beyond final-answer accuracy or success on predefined question-answering tasks.

## Planned Work

- capability-level evaluation;
- process and trajectory evaluation;
- novelty, value, testability, and evidence quality;
- intervention and revision quality;
- transfer across tasks and domains;
- efficiency, controls, and external validation;
- benchmarks and evaluation protocols.

Evaluation work may be released independently and does not require a single unified DFM implementation.
