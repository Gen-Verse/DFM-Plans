# Experimenter

**Status: Planned**

This direction studies how discovery systems choose informative actions, interventions, tools, simulations, or experiments that distinguish between competing explanations.

## DFM Capabilities

- Intervention & Experimentation
- Active information acquisition
- Digital, simulation, and physical grounding

## Planned Work

- intervention and experiment proposal;
- tool-using discovery agents;
- simulation-grounded exploration;
- Dry/Wet experimental workflows;
- evaluation of information gain, cost, and experimental validity.

Projects in this direction may use different environments and do not need to share one execution framework.
