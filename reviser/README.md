# Reviser

**Status: Planned**

This direction studies how models revise problems, representations, hypotheses, and research plans when new external evidence contradicts or weakens the current explanation.

## DFM Capabilities

- Evidence-Grounded Revision
- Verification and falsification
- Failure attribution

## Planned Work

- evidence and verifier design;
- experimental gating;
- counterexample and contradiction handling;
- localized failure attribution;
- revision traces and revision-quality evaluation.

Reviser projects may operate on independent tasks, artifacts, and evidence sources.
