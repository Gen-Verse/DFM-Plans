# Training

**Status: Planned**

This direction studies how discovery-oriented capabilities can be formed through data construction, post-training, reinforcement learning, process supervision, and interaction with scientific environments.

## Planned Work

- discovery trajectories and training data;
- supervised and preference-based post-training;
- scientific and agentic reinforcement learning;
- process rewards, verifiers, and credit assignment;
- curriculum and capability formation;
- data, model, and training infrastructure releases.

Training projects may target one or several DFM research directions and may use different model families or training stacks.
